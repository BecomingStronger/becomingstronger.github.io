# Design: Assassin 33 A.D. dossier

This page copies the look of a straight-to-video poster and trailer. It does not use the site's main design system.

- Fonts: Cinzel (titles), EB Garamond (body), Oswald (billing block), Share Tech Mono (HUD labels). All SIL OFL, from Google Fonts.
- Palette tokens: `--void #07060a`, `--gold #c9a24a`, `--ember #ff7a1a`, `--lab #4fe3ff`, `--nv #39ff88`.
- Spacing: 8px scale.
- Radius: 4px.
- Allowed effects: gold text gradient, sunrise and dome radial glows, lens-flare streak, film grain, scanlines, and text drop shadows. Do not add other gradients or shadows.
- Laurel icon: Lorc, game-icons.net, CC BY 3.0.
